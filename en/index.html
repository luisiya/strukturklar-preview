Companies and organisations today face a particular challenge: limited resources, rising demands, increasing complexity and a dynamic competitive environment.

What matters, therefore, is not only managing existing tasks. What matters is understanding interrelationships, using resources wisely and setting the right priorities.

StrukturKlar makes complex economic and organisational situations understandable and creates a sound basis for better decisions and sustainable development.

When StrukturKlar makes sense

When individual problems are part of a bigger picture.

Perhaps you are facing an important business decision. Perhaps processes no longer work the way they should. Perhaps costs have risen, resources are scarce or responsibilities are unclear.

Often the real challenge does not lie in a single problem, but in the interrelationships between different areas.

StrukturKlar helps you make these interrelationships visible and examine them systematically.

Typical starting situations

  • You want to start a business or develop an existing business activity further.
  • You have to manage limited financial and human resources economically.
  • You want to compare several locations, offers or investment options.
  • You know that processes can be improved, but not yet exactly where the causes lie.
  • You want to identify risks before they turn into bigger problems.
  • You need a structured basis for an important decision.
  • You want to use existing resources more purposefully and identify new development opportunities.

The goal: not just looking at individual symptoms, but understanding the entire starting situation.

How I work

From the facts to a basis for decision-making.

Every question is different. The analytical approach, however, follows a clear structure.

  1. 01

    Understand the IST (as-is)

    What actually exists? What information, figures and framework conditions are available?

  2. 02

    Recognise interrelationships

    How are processes, tasks, people, resources and information connected?

  3. 03

    Define the goal

    What should be achieved, improved or changed?

  4. 04

    Identify the GAP

    What differences exist between the current and the desired situation?

  5. 05

    Examine risks

    Which factors can influence or hinder achieving the goal?

  6. 06

    Develop options

    What different courses of action are there?

  7. 07

    Consider the impact

    What economic, organisational and practical effects may arise?

  8. 08

    Create a basis for decision-making

    The results are structured comprehensibly and prepared in a suitable form.

Good decisions do not come from individual figures. They come from understanding how those figures are connected.

Preparing decisions

A good decision starts with the right questions.

Not every business question starts with a finished business plan. Often it starts with a specific situation, several open questions and different possibilities.

StrukturKlar helps you to organise the question, analyse the relevant factors and make possible courses of action comparable.

Example: You are planning to start a business.

  • What financial prerequisites are in place?
  • What running costs will arise?
  • What resources are needed?
  • What investments need to be considered?
  • Which location or premises option fits the economic framework?
  • What risks and dependencies exist?
  • What information is still missing for a well-founded decision?

Example: You want to improve existing processes.

  • Where do delays or unnecessary work steps occur?
  • What information is missing?
  • Where are responsibilities or interfaces unclear?
  • What resources are tied up?
  • What structural changes might make sense?

Example: You have to choose between several options.

  • Which criteria are relevant for the comparison?
  • What costs and effects arise?
  • Which risks distinguish the options?
  • Which option best fits the available resources and goals?

The result is not a guarantee of success, but a comprehensible analytical basis that supports your own decision-making.
