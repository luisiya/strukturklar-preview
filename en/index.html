How I think

What it is about

StrukturKlar analyses complex organisational and economic relationships, makes structures visible and develops decision and solution models from them.

StrukturKlar combines business experience, process analysis, organisational development and conceptual thinking to make complex relationships visible, comparable and open to further development.

We analyse companies and organisations as systems.

  1. Figures→
  2. Processes→
  3. People→
  4. Resources→
  5. Risks→
  6. Decisions→
  7. Future

Not a single problem, but the connections between its elements.

Core principle

How I think

  1. 1

    AS-IS

    What is actually there?

  2. 2

    STRUCTURE

    How are tasks, processes, people, resources and information connected?

  3. 3

    GOAL

    What should be achieved or changed?

  4. 4

    GAP

    What is missing? Where are the deviations?

  5. 5

    RISK

    What could prevent the goal from being achieved?

  6. 6

    OPTIONS

    What different courses of action are available?

  7. 7

    IMPACT

    What economic, organisational and practical consequences do the options have?

Good decisions do not come from individual figures, but from understanding how they relate to each other.

Preparing decisions

Support for business decisions

StrukturKlar supports the economic and organisational preparation of business decisions.

The focus is on structuring the question, analysing the relevant factors, developing options and assessing their possible effects.

  • Resources

  • Comparing offers

  • Basis for decisions
  • Preparing for business meetings

I support the economic and organisational preparation of business meetings and decisions.

Typical questions

  • Does a particular location suit the business model?
  • What costs actually arise before the start?
  • Which investments are necessary?
  • Which resources are needed?
  • Which tasks have to be taken on personally?
  • Which option makes more economic sense?
  • What risks does a decision create?
  • What information is still missing?
  • What happens if costs, staffing needs or time required turn out differently than planned?

Across several steps

Analytical project support

Not every business question can be answered in a single analysis.

StrukturKlar can support complex decision-making and change processes across several steps – from the first structuring of a question to the development and assessment of concrete options for action.

The focus is not on carrying out individual specialist services, but on analysis, structuring and conceptual support.

Getting started

30-minute StrukturKlar Check

One complex question – structured together.

Do you have a business, organisational or economic question, but are not yet sure where the actual problem lies?

In the StrukturKlar Check, the initial situation is structured first. Together we clarify which information is relevant, which questions need to be examined in more depth and whether further analysis makes sense.

30 minutes, online via video call or by phone.

The check serves to

  1. 1understand the initial situation
  2. 2structure the question
  3. 3identify relevant information and open questions
  4. 4make first connections visible
  5. 5clarify whether further analysis makes sense
  6. 6where applicable, establish whether the question lies outside the scope of services offered

The StrukturKlar Check is not a full consultancy service, not individual legal advice and not unrestricted tax advice.

Describe your question

Do you have a complex question that you would like to structure first?

A short description of the question is enough. After an initial assessment, we can clarify whether the matter fits within the scope of StrukturKlar.
