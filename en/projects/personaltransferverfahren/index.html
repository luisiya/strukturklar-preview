Effective staff transfer procedures

Conceptual model for the competency-based assignment of existing staff resources to organisational needs.

Conceptual model / practical projectResources & staffCompetenciesOrganisation

01Question

Why does a company sometimes need not more staff, but a better allocation of its existing resources?

02Model / method

  1. 1

    Existing staff resources

    • Competencies
    • Experience
    • Qualifications
    • Previous tasks
    • Development potential
    • Costs
    • Capacity
  2. 2

    Organisational needs

    • Tasks
    • Functions
    • Required competencies
    • Capacities
    • Priorities
    • Time requirements
  3. 3

    Matching

  4. 4

    Gap

  5. 5

    Possible options

    • Transfer
    • Development
    • Redistribution
    • New hiring
    • Another organisational solution
  6. 6

    Expected impact

    • Better use of resources
    • Fewer competency gaps
    • Better distribution of tasks
    • Greater transparency
    • Possible reduction of unnecessary staff shortages
