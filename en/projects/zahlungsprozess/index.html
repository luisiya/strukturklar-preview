Optimising payment processing and minimising risk

Instead of reacting only when invoices arrive, creating early transparency about what will become relevant for payment.

Conceptual solution proposal / case studyProcesses & organisationRiskControl structures

01Initial situation

The starting point was a specific problem in the area of payment processing.

Payment processes can depend heavily on individual people and individual processing steps. This can lead to delays, a lack of transparency and risks.

02Question

How can transparency about expected payments be created in advance, instead of reacting only after individual invoices have arrived?

  • Where do delays occur?
  • Which people does the process depend on?
  • What information is missing?
  • Where do control risks arise?
  • Which interfaces are critical?
  • What information could be available earlier?
  • How can process reliability be increased?

03Model / method

Structured solution approach:

  1. As-is→
  2. Risk analysis→
  3. Interfaces→
  4. Control points→
  5. Improvement→
  6. Transparency

04Status

Conceptual solution proposal / case study, not an implemented client project.
