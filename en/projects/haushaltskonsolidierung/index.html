Budget consolidation & resource management

Looking at tasks, resources and structures in a way that makes savings potential visible without losing sight of an organisation's ability to function.

Conceptual workFinancial managementPublic administrationResources

01Initial situation

The question was how public resources can be used more economically in the long term without losing the ability of organisational structures to function.

Tasks, resources, staff, structures and possible change processes were considered together.

02Question

How can tasks, resources and structures be looked at in a way that makes savings potential visible without losing sight of an organisation's ability to function?

03Analysis

Analysis of:

  • tasks
  • staff
  • resources
  • structures
  • processes
  • income
  • expenditure
  • responsibilities
  • organisational dependencies
  • options for change

04Model / method

Development of a conceptual approach for:

  • analysing existing tasks and structures
  • reviewing resource requirements
  • identifying possible savings and optimisation potential
  • considering shifts in staff and tasks
  • linking cost-effectiveness and ability to function
  • developing a systematic view of resources and impact

05Impact

Reducing costs does not automatically mean spending less. What matters is the impact a change has on the system as a whole.

If figures are published later, they will be labelled as: concept, calculation, expected impact, observed impact or proven impact.

06Status

Conceptual work. The work is to be understood as a conceptual model or an approach to thinking and analysis, not as an implemented consolidation programme.
