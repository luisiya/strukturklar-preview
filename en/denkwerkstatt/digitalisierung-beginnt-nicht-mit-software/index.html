Digitalisation does not start with software

When a process does not work, the first step is often to look for a digital solution. However, software cannot automatically improve a poorly structured process.

May 20, 2026Processes, data and digitalisation

01Main idea

Analysis comes before digitalisation:

  • What happens today?
  • What information is needed?
  • Where do errors occur?
  • What interfaces are there?
  • What data is available?
  • What requirements must a digital solution meet?

02Model

  1. Process→
  2. Information→
  3. Data→
  4. Requirements→
  5. Digital solution→
  6. Measurable impact

03Goal

Not simply digitalising, but digitalising sensibly.
