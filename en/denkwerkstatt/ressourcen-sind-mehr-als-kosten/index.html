Resources are more than costs

In organisations, staff are often seen primarily as a cost factor. At the same time, people have experience, knowledge, skills, qualifications and development potential.

July 8, 2026Competency-based resource allocation

01Main idea

The key question is not only: “How many positions do we have?”

But: “What competencies and capacities are available – and where are they actually needed?”

02Model

  1. Existing resources→
  2. Competencies→
  3. Organisational needs→
  4. Matching→
  5. Gap→
  6. Options for action

03Example

An organisation has employees with different experience and competencies, while certain skills are lacking in other areas.

Instead of building up new resources only, it can first be examined whether existing competencies can be deployed differently, developed further or combined.

04Goal

Not only managing resources, but deploying and developing them sensibly.
