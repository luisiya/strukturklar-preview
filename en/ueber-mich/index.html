About me

From figures to structures. From structures to decisions.

About me

My professional path combines economics, finance, accounting, auditing, controlling, public administration and process analysis.

I have worked in a range of organisational settings – from smaller organisations and audit-related work to larger companies and public structures.

Along the way, my focus has increasingly shifted from handling individual cases to the question: how do structures work as a whole – and how can they be made clearer, more economical and more effective?

My academic background is in economics and international finance. It is complemented by many years of practical experience in finance and accounting, controlling, administrative processes, resource management, analysis and quality assurance.

I am currently extending my profile with business analysis, business processes, requirements engineering, data analysis, SQL, Power BI, Python, AI and project management.
