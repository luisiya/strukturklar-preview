About me

I am Anastasiia Gurina – a finance and controlling expert with an academic background in international economics and finance and many years of professional experience in finance, accounting, controlling and organisational management processes.

My professional career began in Ukraine in 1996. Germany has been my home since 2003, and I have continuously developed my professional qualifications and experience here.

My professional positions include work in finance and accounting, responsibility for general ledger and fixed asset accounting processes, support for various corporate structures, and tasks in controlling and municipal administration.

In my work to date, I have, among other things:

  • been responsible for and coordinated financial processes and closing work;
  • managed extensive client portfolios with different requirements;
  • prepared monthly closings, evaluations and management reports;
  • analysed financial and organisational interrelationships;
  • worked with controlling, management, administration and external specialist bodies;
  • coordinated staff professionally and structured work processes;
  • developed concepts and decision-making foundations for economic and organisational questions.

What has shaped me most is the combination of financial analysis and organisational thinking: figures never stand in isolation. They reflect processes, decisions, resources and how they interact.

Academic and professional background

International economics and finance

Specialist Diploma in Economics and Finance (Diplom-Spezialistin), Kyiv, 2006. Academic degree with honours; credential evaluation by the Central Office for Foreign Education (ZAB). In Germany, the degree is classified at bachelor's level.

Tax clerk (Steuerfachangestellte)

Professional qualification in the German tax field, 2015.

Professional experience

Many years of work in finance, accounting, controlling and administration, including tasks in group structures and municipal organisations.

Continuous development

In development / further training

Economic interrelationships change. Analytical methods and digital possibilities are also constantly evolving.

That is why I continuously expand my knowledge, particularly in data management, data-based process analysis, reporting and digital evaluation methods.

Further topics such as SQL, data quality and business intelligence applications are part of my ongoing professional development.

My aim: to combine economic experience with analytical thinking and new methodological possibilities.
