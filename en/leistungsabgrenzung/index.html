Scope of services

StrukturKlar focuses on analytical, conceptual and organisational questions and on structuring and evaluating business and administrative information.

Services are provided solely within the limits permitted by law in each case.

Individual legal advice or unrestricted tax advice within the meaning of the applicable statutory provisions is not offered.

Where work is carried out in the area of bookkeeping or other tax-related workflows, this is done solely within the applicable statutory powers and requirements.

The specific scope is clarified transparently before an assignment begins.

What StrukturKlar offers

  • Business analysis
  • Organisational analysis
  • Structural analysis
  • Process analysis
  • Process modelling
  • Resource analysis
  • Key figure and cost-effectiveness analysis
  • Risk analysis
  • Scenario analysis
  • Conceptual development
  • Decision support
  • Analytical project support
  • Data and digitalisation analysis at the current stage of development

What is not offered

  • no unrestricted tax advice
  • no individual legal advice
  • no representation in legal or tax matters
  • no services beyond the powers permitted by law in each case

Legal and tax questions

If possible legal or tax questions arise during an analysis, this will be pointed out. They are clarified in cooperation with tax advisers or lawyers.
